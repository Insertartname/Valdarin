Sol

"Bring your truth to the light."

Title The Sunweaver, The Sun Hatcher, The Primordial Light
Symbol Hands with sun behind it
Domains Sun, Summer, Argiculture, Truth

Pronouned Sol

Lore

The twin to Solana, Sol is a stern but fair deity. Watching the battle between his mother and father, she lost some of her optimism and naivety of the world. She shines her warmth on those needing purpose, pushing them towards a brigher future while burning those who cannot face their truth.

Depiction

In her natural form Sol is a dragon a golden bird-like dragon that has flames covering their form. In her humanoid state she wears a white cloak that conceals much of her face and body. She has a warm smile, but when the cloak is removed she is a blinding figure with a burning star for a head.

Personality

Blunt but kind, Sol treats species with gentleness unless given reason not to. She listens to Solana's stories of love and exchanges stories of the truth setting someone free.
