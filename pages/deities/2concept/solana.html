Solana

"The moonlight loves and redeems"

Title The Moonweaver, The Lovers friend, The Redeemer
Symbol Hands with crescent moon behind it
Domains Illusion, Love, Moonlight, Night, Tides

Pronouned So-lan-a

Lore

Love and protector of the trysts of lovers. Those who work in secrecy and trickery often ask for her blessing. The Moonweaver sends her will as the goddess of moonlight and the autumn season, as well as the patron of illusions and misdirection. Many consider the Moonweaver to be the goddess of love. It is very difficult to encounter lovers on a tryst, as the Moonweaver creates the shadows for these secret meetings to take place. Those who work in darkness and trickery often ask for her blessing.

Depiction

In her natural form Solana is a white dragon with translucent blue wings.Solana's most often depcited huamnoid form is an adult woman with black and white hair, deep-blue skin like the ocean, twin moons for eyes, a warm smile, and a crescent moon on her forehead. She wears a glowing white silk gown.

Personality

A sincere idealist, Solana enjoys talking about romance and all that comes with it. Some may consider her chaotic good due to her illusions but she does it to protect love.
