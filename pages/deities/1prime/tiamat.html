Tiamat

"Fear the multi-colored scales"

Title The Destoryer, The Scaled Mother, The Dragon Queen, Queen of Chaos, God with Six Heads, The Archqueen of the Nine Hells
Symbol Dragon head with 6 claw marks
Domains Creation and Trickery

Pronouned Ba-ha-mut

Lore

One of the first beings born from the primeval chaos. Alongside her husband, Bahamut, they created all realities. The void came to snuff out their creations, and the dragons engaged in war. Tiamat was deeply wounded, and the infection, the desire for destruction rather than creation, had plagued her mind. The wife attacked her own husband, and without any other choice, he imprisoned her in the lower planes until he could find a cure. His tears created the rivers while her screams formed mountains. He forged metallic dragons after the strengths of his wife, while she created chromatic dragons after his flaws.

Depiction

Tiamat's form has been changed throughout time. When she and Bahamut were lovers, she took the form of a feathery black dragon with red claws, whiskers larger than planets, 5 tails, and four massive wings with red scales. Her fight with Bahamut gravely wounded her, splitting her head into 6 different chromatic dragons, and she had a long tail tipped with a venomous stinger. Her humanoid form is an impossibly beautiful lady with black clothing, black angel wings with red-tipped feathers, black horns, completely black eyes, red halo, and long dark hair.

Personality

Tiamat is arrogant, cold, calculated, and spiteful. She loves her chromatic children and sees mortals as disposable tools to help her escape. Similar to Bahamut, she despises romantic cheaters and will send polymorphed dragons or demons to test mortals.
