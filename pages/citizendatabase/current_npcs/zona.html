Zona Lim'dari is a wanted criminal facing charges including: kidnapping, chronomancy without a license, manipulation of time, manipulation of space, dimensional travel without a license, trafficking across dimensional planes, and more.

History

Our records have little information on Zona, they are an emotionless nephilim who powers are linked to Jocasta, The Galaxy Painter. They are able to manipulate time and space, creating rifts points between history. We have placed multiple chronomancers in different points in time in case they attempt to disrupt. Their most common ability is stopping time itself. We are unsure how they are able to cast Time Stop multiple times a day or how they can hold it for longer than 6 seconds. Researchers are looking into this ability. We are certain they were born in Heavena and had a rather simple life. How they got into contact with Yandreak Wildmight still isn't clear.

Cozy Days, Wishful Nights

On Solfa 4th, 2305 Zona impersonated as a Heavenian government worker looking for Div_9. Astria Lighstone attempted to decieve them. Zona found their target and casted Time Stop by unfurling their wings and shining a bright light. The group entered into a cave where they found a hidden lab with tubes of students, adults, and in a small container Div_9. While in combat Ledo destoryed the computer and tank, freeing Div_9. Alanbreaks the console causing a self destruct system. This forces Astria to use her ultimatium, morphing into a dragon to save 7 kids while her students are tasked with saving 14 adults. Zona using magic takes 5, Div_9 puts three into its form, and the students save the rest. Zona hasn't been seen since.
