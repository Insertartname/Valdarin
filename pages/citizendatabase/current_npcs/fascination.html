Fascination is a tielfing born in Struilia but moved after her city was destroyed during the Blight Wars. She now attends Astria's after school program and looks up to her.

Death Blossom

In New Struilian the Blight attacked and sent hordes of the undead. The Wild Hunt helped but many buildings such as the Mage Council were destroyed. Fascination was saved by Astria and personally thanked her. After the Blight's defeat, Fascination begged her family to move to Heavena to be closer to her hero. Hesitant they took some time for research and made the decision to move to Nicodemus.

Cozy Days, Wishful Nights

After school Fascination, Ledo, andAlan arrived Astria's after school program for the troubled youth. Is Fascination troubled? No, she just really wants to near her hero.A stranger knocked on the door, Zona, and used chonomagic to stop time. Kidnapping Div_9, Fascination, and several kids Astria chased after them with some students. The group entered into a cave where they found a hidden lab with tubes of students, adults, and in a small container Div_9. While in combat Ledo destoryed the computer and tank, freeing Div_9. Alan breaks the console causing a self destruct system. This forces Astria to use her ultimatium, morphing into a dragon to save 7 kids while her students are tasked with saving 14 adults. Zona using magic takes 5, Div_9 puts three into its form, and the students save the rest. Flying them all back to Nicodemus, they all receive another vision:

a cave, below with nemite, a celestial metal that only grows in heavena. deep within a cave system is nothing but a door...this door opens and we see a woman covered in runes. She's found Experiment Con#9_536-10.

Additonally, Astria notices small feathery angel wings growing from Fascination ankles.
