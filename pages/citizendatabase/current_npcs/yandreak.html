An extremely dangerous and intelligent individual, Yandreak Wildmight was the Header Researcher at the Mage Council before he was fired.

History

Born in Astra, Yandreak was an excellent military scientist for the Astran Army. In collaboration with Odron military contracts, he helped create Warships with anti-magical fields, BULK droppers, wyvern containers, and many high-tech weaponry. The circumstances are unclear but he left the military and joined the Mage Council. During his time he got several PHDs related to the Weave, Planar Dimensions, Dimensional Travel, and History. He advanced our knowledge on psionics, chonomancy, graviturgy, and technomancy while never being a mage. He studied the planes and how it interacted with the material planes. He was deeply interested in the humanoid variants touched by the elemental planes known as Gensai. During the Blight Wars, Yandreak became more obessive over the concept of 10th level spells or higher and was placed on leave due to mental health. He never returned.
