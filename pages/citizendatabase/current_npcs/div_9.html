An experiment of Yandreak Wildmight, Div_9 is a purple ooze with big yellow eyes. Divination marks repeat along its form and seems to enjoy consuming knowledge.

Cozy Days, Wishful Nights

Slipping into Astria's home and hiding in a bookshelf, Div_9 gave her a vision. 14 gentle eyes followed by 20 draconic eyes in different chromatic colors bathed in hellfire, the words "I'm sorry" from an unknown voice, and a tear in the sky. The following day Astria took it to her job of teaching troubled youth. A stranger knocked on the door, Zona, and used chonomagic to stop time. Kidnapping Div_9, Fascination, and several kids Astria chased after them with some students. The group entered into a cave where they found a hidden lab with tubes of students, adults, and in a small container Div_9. While in combat Ledo destoryed the computer and tank, freeing Div_9. Alanbreaks the console causing a self destruct system. This forces Astria to use her ultimatium, morphing into a dragon to save 7 kids while her students are tasked with saving 14 adults. Zona using magic takes 5, Div_9 puts three into its form, and the students save the rest. Flying them all back to Nicodemus, they all receive another vision
