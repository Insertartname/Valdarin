Main Quests

Prerequisite: N/A

Given by: Charlette Roosevelt of the Freelancers

Objective: Find Con_9

Description: Con_9 has been located below Heavena in the Underdark. We are unsure of the depth below, but we have observed physical details: Nemite, a celestial metal that grows below the Middle or Lower Dark; a large wooden door with a brass handle; and the soft blue glow of native beetles, known as beeticals.

This mission is a simple search and locate with standard resistance. We expect a challenge rating of 3 to 4. There have been rumors of a dragonborn cult spotted, but our information indicates that they are non-hostile. As long as adventurers do not touch dragon bones, they should be safe from them.

Task: Each adventurer will be given a teleportation stone. Once Con_9 has been located, place stones in a circular formation and place one of your guild IDS in the center. This will activate the teleportation, allowing other freelancers to quick-travel over. We want to establish communication with Con_9 and indicate we are friendly while protecting them from others, such as Yandreak Wildmight's potential forces.

Reward: 20 gold and 30 silver each, rank up to Pearl II

Written by: Hailey Kim

Classification: Main campaign quest, starter
