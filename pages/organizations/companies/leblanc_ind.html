History

Leblanc Industries is an Odron multiregional corporation and technology company headquartered in Terbet. It is best known for consumer electronics, software, and services. Founded by the Leblanc Brothers to produce arcane infused personal computers, the Blanc I revolutionized devices. While many companies had tried and failed to incorporate magic into microchips, Leblanc industries found the most effienct method. Leblanc's prodcut lineup include: phones, tablets, watches, laptops, Tvs, software, and electric vehicles. With this money Alfredo started the Leblanc Foundation to fight against poverty in the underdark of Odron.

Aflredo's Death

The company fell silent for a few years after the murder of Alfredo Leblanc, his wife Myrrah Leblanc, and Cassandra Leblanc went missing. Brandon Leblanc returned to the technology industry with more determination than ever to assist poverty. He built parks, recreational centers, and innovanted his technology to make it as consumer friendly as possible.
