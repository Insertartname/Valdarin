History

The Ancient Apex is a corporate guild that promotes wrestling, technogical enhancements, and unity. There are two teams Dino-Genes (DG) and Dino-Fighters (DF). DG are wrestlers who use their shapeshifting abilites to become dino-humanoid hybrids similar to lycanthropy. Each member must have a signature move, finisher move, theme song, and always be in character when in public. Each adventure they do is followed by a tour for their show, Initative Wrestling Entertainment (IWE). DF are city-level heroes that use cybernetic suits for enhancements and colossal bio-mechanical robots named Palo-bots. They can combine to make MegaPalo, great for merchandise.
