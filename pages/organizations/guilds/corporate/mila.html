History

MILA is a pop band guild consisting of six members: lead True, main dancer Caeviryn, main rapper Vacena, main vocalist Vi-Ivy, cutie Sadie, badass Margot, and recent baby-faced Liazana. Members are from Fedinia, Astra, Odron, Heavena, Struilia, Depths, and Underdark. The group was formed through a reality competiton series chronciled in their docuseriesPopstar Adventurers: MILA. MILA debuted in June 2300 with their single "Blight", followed by their platinum single, "Magic". They have have released several EPs and are prepared to release an LP, not named yet.
