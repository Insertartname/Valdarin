History

Azure Talons or Maidens of the Blue Flame are women who wield sacred arms and largely fight against plane-traveling threats. While considered a corporate guild due to donations and taking from investors, they largely maintain their autonomy. To join the sisterhood, you must take a vow to Istavel, the Duality Balancer. Azure Kayxina distributes these vows to her sisters and pairs them for missions. Each is stationed in settlements of Fedinia to protect against elemental plane tears or contracted by other regions to fight against demons.
