History

The Silver Order is the longest standing guild in Heavena and was created during the Tielfing Trials. They are dedicated to Bahamut, the Justice Bringer and spread his worship. While having a dark history after the formation of the United Council they transitioned to helping additonal regions. Similar to the Azure Talons, each member takes an oath for protecting Heavenian citizen. The newest champion is Flora Watcher, a member who helped during the Blight War. The previous Champion Jeremiah Starblossom sadly passed during the war.
