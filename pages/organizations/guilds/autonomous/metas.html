History

The Metas, originally known as the Dragons of the Crest, are the strongest guild in modern history and the second strongest guild in history. Dragonborne families founded and dominated in Guild Wars, using their magical items to complete difficult missions. Only the strongest can pass their exam and be considered a member. Wizards with powerful magic combos, warriors with magical items, druids with wildshape beyond many, and multiclassers seeking the highest potential in themselves may join. They have never lost to a guild outside of Color Theory and still hold a grudge against all their members. The guild has continued to innovate by pairing the most powerful spells, items, and abilities to defeat missions with ease.

The guild doesn't associate with many other adventurers outside of the Freelancers. All other guilds are seen as beneath them.
