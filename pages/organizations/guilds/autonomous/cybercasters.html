History

The Cybercasters is the first arcane guild to be founded in Struilia. The organizations was founded to explore the intersection between magic and technology. They believe technology is itself magic, and that advancements in technology means advancement for every species. Robotic enhancements, AI partners, and technomancy all were invented or adopted by this guild. While BMP founded the guild, all staff have an equal say when it comes to decision making. Additionally they provide resources back to their community and work with other Struilian guilds to help distrubite wealth.
