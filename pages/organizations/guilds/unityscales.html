History

The oldest guild on the Material Plane, Unity Scales was created when Maerien became a Warden to those interested in draconic combat. A Warden is a variant of Dragons that hoard people instead of treasures. She taught her knights the techniques of fighting like a dragon; giving her members capes that can become wings, weapons, artifacts, and armor. When adventure guilds became more common Maerin entered into a war state, capturing any guild beneath her to join. They were the strongest guild until the Metas arrived with powerful magic items.

Once accepted into the guild, each member is given a wyvern as their partner. These wyverns known as Units have the special ability to transform into powerful weapons. Additionally, Units can combine into massive dragons.
