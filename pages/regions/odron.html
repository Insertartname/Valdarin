History

Established as an underground region, Odron came to the surface during the early modern era and advanced humanoids into the technology boom. Through dwarfish ideals, Odron created: Warforge, Bulks, airships, cars, and many other inventions we still use today. Unfortunately during the Great Waaugh War, they created the Mag-bomb, a nuclear weapon capable of wiping nations off the world. The United Council was created in response and banned such technoloy. Odron transitioned to more peaceful tech such as internet, surveillance tools, and mech sports. The region is now split in two - the surface and underground (knonw as low-dron), access is through the metro.
