Episode 1 - Give Hope

- The group begins the mission and are attacked by a hobgoblin and a group of goblins.

- Esther tricks a goblin with an illusion of a phantasmal "hot goblin lady." (First move in the new campaign!)

- Hobgoblin Leader attempts to cut the bridge Azrael was standing on. (He held on.)

- Hobgoblin Leader attempts to run away and seal the mouth of the cave.

- The party defeats the hobgoblin and ends combat before entering thew deeper parts of the cave. It takes 1 hour to travel

- Ezrik lights a lantern fore those who cannot see in the dark.

- The party comes across a giant metal door that's asking for an access key. Strangely enough, there is ruined pillars and foliage despite the party getting closer to the Underdark

- Azrael used his Adventurer's Guild ID and the giant door opened with purple smoke spewing forth

- Party enters a cave system. Phone connection dies. A choice is presented. They follow the path with a melodic and constant sound.

- The party encounters a mining Kobold. He seems friendly.

- Party ends up fumbling and saying too much information about killing the goblins. Kobold panics. Esther blatantly says the goblins are dead.

- Party barely manages to calm down the Kobold and them their objective. Kobold is moreso neutral now rather than friendly.

- Ezrik and Azrael speak in Draconic to the Kobold and the Kobold decides to bring them to the "Star Eyed" Prophet. The party follows.

- The party after 3 hours meets Grando, the Star Eyed Prophet. A dragonborn leading many Kobolds.

- Grando tells the party about their "deity" known as Paradise. She is described to be an elf.

- Ezrik recalls that no such deity exists. He wants to know more. Grando accepts this request.

- Grando offers to give the party a place to rest. They accept most reluctantly.

- Ezrik prays with Grando. Meanwhile, Azrael overheard some painful groans and investigated. He witnessed some sort of sacrificial ritual.

- Azrael huddled with the group and told them what he saw. They must find Ezrik.

- The party went and found Ezrik while Grando was attempting to cast some sort of spell. Next »
