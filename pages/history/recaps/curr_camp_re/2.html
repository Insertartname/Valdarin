Episode 1 - Give Hope

- Grando wipes the blood away from his mouth and rants about making everyone present know and understand paradise.

- Grando claps his hands together, and a flash of light happens; thus, combat begins. However, Azrael hits him with a cool one liner as follows, "Unfortunately for you, all stars fade eventually."

- As the flash of light happens, the party is shown visions of their various pasts and how they joined the Adventurer's Guild.

- Another memory comes into play. The party recalls the day of the Adventurer's Guild Combat Exam and how they each felt that day.

- Through these memories that they recall, the party seems to be exploring how they each passed the Combat Exam. Each story seems to be different, but in reality, they seem to be fighting Grando through the recollection.

- Azrael recalls the goblins from that day and how he scoffed at his would-be fellow adventurers' being overwhelmed by something so insignificant. He punches them, but in actuality, he hit Grando.

- Ezrik recalls how he prayed that day to be given strength through Bahamut and his word, thus casting a spell and burning incoming wolves. In actuality, he set Grando ablaze.

- Pluto recalls how he healed a wounded would-be adventurer and then attempted to cast a spell on a goblin, but it resisted. In actuality, Grando resisted.

- Chesca recalls how he assisted his fellow adventurers by shooting down blink dogs who were too fast for anyone to react to. In actuality, he shot Grando in the shoulder.

- Esther recalls how there were deep gnomes and how they relied on such old magic and old tricks; wanting to spice things up, she cast mirror image to fashion her own makeshift band. Playing her electric violin, she casts prestidigitation to distract the deep gnomes and distract them from using any spells. In actuality, she distracted Grando from his purpose. He rants on about paradise.

- In the heat of battle, Azrael manages to land a critical hit in his memories/on Grando! In his memories, he cleaves through multiple goblins, but in reality, he lands a devastating blow against a cultist and Grando that utterly ruins them. Leaving them crushed within a wall. The strike also destroyed Grando's illusion! Combat ends.

- They entered the room where Azrael originally heard the screams of someone undergoing a form of ritual torture. Azrael kicked down the door.

- Ezrik revives the man who was undergoing the ritualistic torture via lay on hands. They are thankful.

- Azrael frees the other captives.

- We meet a human male named Matt, Abel the dwarf, and Brianna the elf.

- They explain how they were captured and the lack thereof memory of it.

-The party hears the cultists chanting about paradise, Pluto checks on it by listening in and then reporting it. He reports that there is a dragon.

- The captives suddenly disappear in a flash of light.

-The dragon incinerates its followers. It could be heard from within the room.

- A woman named Conthenia, a celestial, enters the room and explains the situation quickly. The cultists were knocked unconscious by her casting illusory dragon fire. Some sort of spell.

- Pluto attempted to prepare a spell, but Conthenia was warping his staff around the room in his relentless pursuit.

- Conthenia asks the party if they work for Yandrake or know who he is.

- Conthenia teleports everyone to her mansion and reveals that she is not a Celestial but an Arcaneborn. She is the person they are looking for.

- Through discussion, the party recalls what they need to do and questions it.

- Through a flashback, they recall who they were going to summon for this guild request. It was Hailey Kim.

- The party asks permission to do what they came to do. Conthenia obliges and allows them to do so.

- They summon Hailey Kim, and she begins to talk with Conthenia.

- They go through a portal back to the guild. Mission completed.

- The party separates to do their own things. Their own ways of relaxing.

- Azrael goes to a bar, Drake challenges him to the pit

- Ezrik goes to the forge area.

- Esther is waiting for her parole officer

- Pluto is in his adventure room, sword pulling out sword and waving it around

- Chesca is cleaning a gun

- They get 120 vales and rank up to Pearl II
