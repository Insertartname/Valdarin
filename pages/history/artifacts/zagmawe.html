History

Zagnith Maw is a powerful artifact given to Zagnith's champion, the one who possibly could ascend to his aspect. The weapon has changed forms throughout its possession, but typically takes the form of a great axe that is made from an unknown black alloy. The twin blade curves outward, then down at a point. When in use, the tips of the blades grow a neon green that resembles flames, and orcish eyes glow red in the dancing heat. As blood touches the blade, its sharpness and durability increase, adding to its power.

The weapon was first used by Zagnith himself during the first Great Waugh War of 2010 - 2025. After Kaiva's defeat, Zagnith's rampage ceased for only 3 years before he had a new goal: to become a deity. Through brute force, he put together a small army and began raiding settlements. Those captured would either join his army or be his slaves, forced to pray and worship him. Once he reached enough power, Zagnith became Ascended and, with his newfound immortality, more brutal. He gained thousands of worshippers and eventually challenged a deity for the domain of war. He won; the previous deity's name cannot be spoken anymore. Any attempt to say their name causes a pause as if you stopped speaking.

Now a the peak of power, Zagnith gave his best warrior his old weapon and gave it multiple boons. Zagnith was close to capturing Odron when they created the Mag-bomb, a devastating combination of magic and technology. The Mag-bomb provided an advantage to Odron and allowed them to push them back until a second bomb was dropped in 2066 to destroy the capital of Kaiva. Zagnith's champion died but resurrected as an LTE (Lament the end), a giant skeletal warrior that required an additional bomb to take out by itself.

We believe Zagnith has found a new champion, but we have not found them yet.
