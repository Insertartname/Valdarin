Soul coins are magical currency of the Nine Hells. They are composed of a hellish iron called inferno steel. They are large coins measuringg 5 in (13 cm) in width and 1 in (2.5 cm) in thickness. They usually weighed about 0.33 lb (150 g). One the front facing side etched onto its surface is an abstract and the back has an infernal script. Soul coins are made by compressing a human soul into a coin through hellish magic. The soul is tormented while in the coin, the more despair, the more its worth. Non-evil creatures are only able to carry a few coins at a time while evil creatures can carry as many as they wish.

The coin possess innate powers that are quite powerful. The coin could be used to drain the life of a targeted creature or communicate with the soul within telepathically. It can answer quesitons but often it is vague and they seemed tortured to do so. It is possible to release the soul within a coin but it is extremely difficult.

Yandreak Wildmight has figured out another purpose. Somehow he can use the soul coin as a binder to create Arcaneborn.
